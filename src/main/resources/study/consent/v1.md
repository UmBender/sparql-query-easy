You are invited to take part in a study of SPARQL EasyQuery, a tool for querying knowledge graphs by drawing the query.

- What you do: answer a short profile questionnaire, solve a few proposed queries in the editor, and answer a usability questionnaire.
- What is stored: your answers to the questionnaires, the queries you submit with their timing and correctness, and the time you accepted this text. No name, e-mail or contact is asked.
- Who sees it: only the study's researchers; results are reported without identifying anyone.
- Taking part is voluntary. If you decline, nothing is stored and you can still use the editor.
